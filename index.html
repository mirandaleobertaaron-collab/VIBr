How to read this model

The model applies a position-dependent standing-wave force to spherical particles and balances it against Stokes drag. Starting positions are distributed across the chamber height; particle movement is integrated over the estimated residence time.

The 25 µm mesh opening and upper/lower pressure-node pattern are assumptions. The mesh is smaller than the modeled 50 µm particles, so it may mechanically retain particles after contact. The model omits acoustic streaming and detailed three-dimensional flow and sound fields.

Results are theoretical estimates, not measured removal efficiency. The graph uses 20,001 modeled starting positions; the chamber animation shows representative particles.
